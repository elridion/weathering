# Weathering

Valheim mod that lets player terrain edits outside player bases heal: grass grows back into dirt and
cultivated paint from its edges, height edits erode (diffusion of the offset field), each spot 3 in-game days after its last edit.
Stack, build, verification and git workflow are shared by all mods: see `../CLAUDE.md` and
`../.claude/rules/`. `docs/design.md` is the spec, `docs/plan.md` the task list.

## Where things are

- `src/WeatheringPlugin.cs` binds the config, applies the Harmony patches, adds the heal runner and
  registers the console commands.
- `src/TerrainPatches.cs` stamps edited vertices on the zone owner; `src/StampStore.cs` keeps the
  stamps, pending flag and last-pass time on the terrain compiler ZDO.
- `src/BaseIndex.cs` finds bases, Warden stones and pieces around a zone from saved ZDOs.
- `src/Healer.cs` is the pure grass-spread (paint) and erosion math.
- `src/TerrainData.cs` decodes and encodes a zone's saved `TCData` without a `TerrainComp`.
- `src/HealRunner.cs` heals flagged zones on the server while nobody is near (`IsZoneIdle` is the
  shared "safe to write this zone" test), and near sleeping players while the night is skipped;
  `src/HealApply.cs` applies such an overnight result on the peer that owns the zone.
  `src/HealCommands.cs` has `weathering_age`,
  `weathering_status` and `weathering_clear_sites`.
- `src/TreePatches.cs` records a regrowth site when a tree is felled; `src/RegrowthSites.cs`
  registers the invisible marker prefab, maps trees to their saplings and writes the site keys.
  `src/RegrowthRunner.cs` sweeps the sites on the server and, once due and idle, drops sites inside
  protected circles or without a sapling, waits on a standing stump, else creates the sapling ZDO.
  `src/PlantPatches.cs` keeps those saplings healthy whatever vanilla's growth checks say.
- `tools/make_icon.ps1` generates `package/icon.png`. `docs/` design notes. `hexium.json` Hexium
  listing metadata.
- The mod has no player-facing strings: no translations, no `AddLocalizations`.

## Invariants that must not break

- **Saved-world compatibility.** Persisted or networked identifiers: plugin GUID
  `elridion.weathering`, ZDO keys `weathering_stamps` (byte[]), `weathering_pending` (bool) and
  `weathering_last` (long, world time in whole seconds of the last heal pass), the regrowth marker
  prefab `weathering_regrowth_site` with ZDO keys `weathering_tree` (int, felled tree prefab hash),
  `weathering_stump` (int, stump prefab hash, 0 if none) and `weathering_felled` (int, StampStore
  minutes, at least 1), the ZDO key `weathering_regrown` (bool, on saplings the mod planted), the
  routed RPC `Weathering_ApplyHeal` (one `ZPackage`), and config
  section/key names (renaming a key resets users' settings). It also reads Warden's
  `spawnward_radius` (float) and prefab `piece_spawnward`; those belong to Warden.
- **Removing the mod must leave a valid world.** Only ever write vanilla-valid `TerrainComp` data.
- **Docs follow code.** `package/README.md` keeps the config table in sync with
  `WeatheringPlugin.Awake`, and its lists of what heals and what never does with the heal rules.
